How this works
  1. Each Sunday, tell us what your week looks like in the box below — type it or record it. Prince plans the filming around your real days.
  2. You film on two mornings a week. Saturday is the main one; the course morning is free footage.
  3. Every post has a step-by-step list. No settings, no angles to learn.
  4. Send the clips to Prince. We edit, caption and schedule. Nothing goes live without you seeing it first.
  5. Went somewhere, did something? Just film it. We'll find a place for it.

The weekly rhythm — the same every week

  1. Sunday — Swatti writes or records her week ahead.
  2. Monday — Prince finalises that week's shots against her real days.
  3. Tuesday morning — course footage, filmed while she's already there.
  4. Saturday morning — the main block, roughly two hours. Covers next week's posts.
  5. Sunday to Tuesday — edit, captions, hashtags.
  6. Wednesday — Swatti approves the cuts. Thursday — scheduled.

Everything is filmed a week before it goes out. That gap is what stops this turning into a panic, and means a bad week costs us nothing.
